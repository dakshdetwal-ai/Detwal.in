Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how DETwal collects, uses and protects information when you use our services.

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the services you use, DETwal may collect information such as your name, email address, account information and information that you voluntarily provide to us.

Trading Journal users may also provide trading-related information such as symbols, entries, exits, risk information, notes and other journal data.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to provide and operate DETwal services, create and manage accounts, provide customer support, improve our products and communicate important service-related information.

3. Account Information

If you create a DETwal account, information associated with your account may be stored to provide account functionality and access to relevant services.

4. Trading Journal Data

Information entered into the Trading Journal may be stored so that you can view, manage and analyze your trading records.

You should avoid entering passwords, payment card information or other highly sensitive information into trading journal notes.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

DETwal may use browser technologies such as cookies, local storage or similar technologies to provide functionality, remember preferences and improve the user experience.

6. Third-Party Services

DETwal may use third-party services to provide certain features, such as hosting, analytics, authentication, payments or community functionality.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. WhatsApp Community

If you choose to join the DETwal WhatsApp community, your interaction with WhatsApp is governed by WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

DETwal does not control WhatsApp's data-processing practices.

8. Payments

If paid services are introduced, payment information may be processed by third-party payment providers. DETwal does not intend to store complete payment card details on its own systems.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain accounts, comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes.

11. Your Choices

Depending on the service and applicable law, you may have rights regarding access, correction or deletion of certain personal information.

12. Children's Privacy

DETwal services are not intended to encourage children to provide personal information without appropriate supervision. If you believe a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page together with an updated effective date.

14. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how DETwal handles information, please contact us through the contact information provided on the website.
